# Lupo Player — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Lupo Player ("the app") is a media player for Android TV, Google TV and Android phones. It plays streams and playlists that you add yourself. The app does not provide any channels, playlists or content of its own.

## What data the app collects

**None.** The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not receive any information about you or about what you watch.

## What is stored on your device

The following stays only on your device:
- the playlists and Xtream Codes logins you add;
- favourites, channel settings and the last channels you watched;
- the TV guide (EPG) cache;
- the parental control PIN;
- the app settings.

This data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Network connections

The app connects only to the addresses that you enter yourself: your playlists, streams and TV guide sources. These services are run by third parties, and their own privacy policies apply to them.

## Backup files

When you create a backup, the app saves an encrypted file to the folder you choose (for example, Downloads). It stays on your device or storage until you move or delete it.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — to play the streams and load the playlists you add.
- **Start at boot** and **display over other apps** — only for the optional "start with the TV" setting.
- **Storage access** — only to find a backup file when you restore settings.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address.

## Contact

You can contact the developer by the email shown on the app's Google Play page.

# Lupo Player — Политика конфиденциальности

Lupo Player — плеер для Android TV, Google TV и телефонов на Android. Он воспроизводит только те плейлисты и потоки, которые вы добавили сами. Своих каналов и контента в приложении нет.

**Приложение не собирает никаких данных.** В нём нет регистрации, рекламы, аналитики и слежения. Разработчик не получает никаких сведений о вас и о том, что вы смотрите.

Плейлисты, избранное, настройки, телепрограмма и ПИН-код родительского контроля хранятся только на вашем устройстве и удаляются вместе с приложением. Приложение подключается только к адресам, которые вы ввели сами. Файл резервной копии зашифрован и сохраняется туда, куда вы выбрали.

Связаться с разработчиком можно по адресу электронной почты, указанному на странице приложения в Google Play.
