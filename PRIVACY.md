# Lupo Player — Privacy Policy

*Version of 11 November 2026*

## 1. Who we are
The operator of Lupo Player (the "App") is Lupo Player. Contact: kobozevhot@gmail.com.

## 2. We do not collect your data
The App has no registration, accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking. The operator has no servers that receive information about you. We do not know who you are, which playlists you added or what you watch.

## 3. What is stored on your device
Stored only on your device: the playlist addresses and credentials you entered (encrypted), favourites, settings, PIN codes (as an irreversible code), recent channels and an error log. This data is not sent to the operator. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

## 4. What the App connects to
- The sources you added yourself (playlists, streams, programme guides). Their owners see your IP address, device information and the credentials you entered for that source. They act under their own policies; we are not responsible for them.
- The version installed from a link checks at start-up for an update on the operator's GitHub page. GitHub sees your IP address under its own policy. No personal data is sent in this check.

## 5. Backup copy
A copy is created only at your request and saved where you choose. It contains playlist addresses and credentials. The copy is protected by the App's own key, not by your personal password, so keep the file in a safe place and do not give it to others.

## 6. Permissions
Internet: for playback. File access: to find and save a backup copy. Autostart and display over other apps: only if you turn autostart on. Install apps: only to update the App.

## 7. Children
The App is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect their data. Parents and guardians are responsible for children's use of the App.

## 8. Your rights
Because the operator holds no data about you, there is nothing for it to provide, correct or delete. You can delete all data yourself by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. For privacy questions, including rights under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law and the EU GDPR, write to kobozevhot@gmail.com.

## 9. Security
Data on the device is protected by system encryption. No storage method is completely secure; look after your device and your backup files.

## 10. Changes
We may update this Policy. The current version is always available in the App's Settings.
